🚀 INÍCIO RÁPIDO
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

WINDOWS:
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. Compilar: gradlew.bat build
2. Configurar: deploy-scripts\setup-deploy.bat
3. Deploy: deploy-scripts\deploy-pterodactyl.bat


LINUX/MAC:
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. Compilar: ./gradlew build
2. Configurar: ./deploy-scripts/setup-deploy.sh
3. Deploy: python3 deploy-scripts/deploy-pterodactyl.py
